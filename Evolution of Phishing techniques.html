Phishing isn't what it used to be.
It's now surgical, invisible, and ruthless.
🔍 While conducting threat analysis on Scattered Spider (UNC3944 / Octo Tempest), I discovered their phishing infrastructure had evolved far beyond what most detection tools expect. Here's what I found about how modern phishing actually works.
We've moved far beyond "click here to claim your prize." Modern phishing is a precision operation — attackers study their targets, fingerprint their visitors, and serve malicious content only to the exact people they want to compromise.

Scattered Spider's adoption of AitM toolkits like Evilginx was a turning point. Here's what that evolution looks like 👇
Conditional Loading
Phishing pages only activate for the right target. Everyone else sees a blank page or a legitimate site.
Precision Targeting
Email is validated against an attacker's database before the login form even appears. No match? No phish.
Visitor Fingerprinting
Screen resolution, timezone, browser plugins — all checked to confirm you're a real victim, not a researcher.
IP Blocking
Security vendors, VirusTotal, urlscan, cloud providers — all blocked before they can even see the page.
One-Shot URLs (Evilginx)
"One IP, one chance." Revisit the URL and you get a fake 404 — or your IP is permanently banned.
AitM Frameworks
Evilginx sits between victim and real login — capturing MFA tokens live, bypassing 2FA entirely. Core to Scattered Spider's 2024–25 campaigns.

The old playbook of "check the URL and don't click suspicious links" is not enough anymore.

Defenders need to think like attackers: assume detection evasion is built in from day one. Zero Trust, phishing-resistant MFA (passkeys, hardware keys), and behavioral anomaly detection aren't optional upgrades — they're table stakes.
